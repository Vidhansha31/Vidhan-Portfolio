## Product

Vitaran is a distribution management system designed to replace spreadsheet-based processes in a family-run milk business. It brings customer-specific pricing, inventory, delivery activity, and expense tracking into a more structured workflow, with separate experiences for administration and delivery workers.

---

## My Role

* **Product Ownership:** Product Owner for the product concept and requirements.
* **Workflow Mapping:** Identified user needs and mapped the administrator and delivery worker workflows.
* **Scope Definition:** Defined product scope around customer-specific pricing, inventory, deliveries, and expenses.
* **Functional Planning:** Planned functional requirements and supported interface and system decisions.

---

## Execution

The product workflow is organized around the day-to-day needs of the business. The administrator manages customer and pricing information, inventory, deliveries, and expenses. Delivery workers use a focused mobile workflow for delivery-related tasks. Invoice communication through WhatsApp is part of the planned business workflow.

The system is being developed with a web application, mobile experience, backend services, and a shared database. The technology context includes Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, AWS, Nginx, and Docker.

---

## Challenges & Achievements

### Challenges

* **Process Modernization:** Replacing spreadsheet-based processes with clear, repeatable workflows.
* **Dynamic Pricing:** Supporting customer-specific pricing without relying on manual exceptions.
* **Role Separation:** Keeping administrative tasks and delivery worker tasks clear and distinct.

### Achievements

* **Unified System Direction:** Defined a product direction for managing pricing, inventory, deliveries, and expenses in one system.
* **Core User Flows:** Mapped core workflows for the administrator and delivery worker.
* **Requirements Translation:** Translated operational needs into product requirements and system decisions.
