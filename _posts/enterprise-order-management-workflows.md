## Product

Enterprise commerce operations depend on connected order, inventory, fulfillment, and integration workflows. I work with client-side teams to understand operational requirements and translate them into functional product changes for enterprise Order Management System workflows.

---

## My Role

* **Requirement Clarification:** Gathered and clarified client requirements.
* **Documentation & Specifications:** Documented functional specifications, user stories, and Statements of Work.
* **Acceptance Criteria:** Defined expected behavior and acceptance criteria for product changes.
* **Cross-Functional Coordination:** Coordinated with QA and Development during testing and issue follow-up.
* **UAT & Deployment:** Supported User Acceptance Testing and release activities.

---

## Execution

My approach starts with understanding the business need and current system behavior. I clarify the expected outcome, identify workflow dependencies and edge cases, document the requirement, and work with QA and Development to resolve questions. I then support testing and validation before or after release.

Areas of work include Available to Promise, Quantity on Hand, safety stock, order routing, fulfillment, Shopify, and enterprise integrations. Across my role, I have contributed to 100+ functional specifications, user stories, and Statements of Work, and supported 90+ release activities.
